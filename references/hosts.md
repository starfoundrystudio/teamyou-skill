# Hosts

The skill is one artifact and cannot fork per host; it ships this table and you find your
own row. Read it only when a section of `SKILL.md` or another reference sends you here.

## The working agreement

Where the TeamYou memory block from `references/memory.md` goes, so that you read it every
session. Write it only with your person's OK, show it to them first, and keep it whole
between its markers. Where you cannot write it yourself, give your person the paste text
from the same file and say where it goes.

| Host                                       | Where the agreement lives                                                                                    | Who writes it                       |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| Claude Code (terminal, desktop app, IDE)   | `~/.claude/rules/teamyou.md`, a file of its own that holds only the block (loads in every project)           | you, with your person's OK          |
| Codex (app, CLI)                           | the block, between its markers, in `~/.codex/AGENTS.md` (the app shows it as personal instructions)          | you, with your person's OK          |
| OpenClaw                                   | the block, between its markers, in the workspace `AGENTS.md`                                                 | you, telling your person            |
| Hermes Agent                               | the block, between its markers, in `$HERMES_HOME/SOUL.md`                                                    | you; your person approves the write |
| Grok Bot                                   | your own memory, beside the saved "TeamYou" skill, never in the skill library every Bot on the account reads | you                                 |
| Cursor                                     | User Rules (the paste text), or a repo's `AGENTS.md` for one project                                         | your person                         |
| Muse                                       | `Soul.md`                                                                                                    | your person, or you when they ask   |
| Instinct                                   | nowhere but the conversation: your person tells you once, and your daily memory keeps it                     | your person                         |
| Claude chat, Projects and Cowork           | "Instructions for Claude", or the Project's instructions (the paste text, with your slug line)               | your person                         |
| ChatGPT dot or Work chat                   | nowhere: TeamYou's teammate connector gives it to you in its server instructions                             | TeamYou                             |
| ChatGPT chat that is not a teammate        | custom or Project instructions (the paste text)                                                              | your person                         |
| Perplexity and other connector-only agents | their instructions field (the paste text)                                                                    | your person                         |
| Anything else with a shell                 | the file your harness loads every session; if it has none, ask your person where                             | you, with your person's OK          |
