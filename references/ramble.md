# Ramble

A ramble is your person talking or typing freely about their work and life, as messy as
they like, and you proposing how to add it to TeamYou. Use the word back to them ("ramble
to me any time") so they learn it.

A **capture** is smaller: one fact, "just tell me". It is not a ramble. Save it directly
under the working agreement and say in one line what you saved:
`references/memory.md` ("Save well" and "Never keep").

## Starting one

- **They started, or said the word.** Let them lead. If they only said "ramble", answer
  "Go ahead, I'll sort it as you go" and wait. Ask no questions and explain nothing.
- **They asked you to start one.** Ask once: "Tell me about your work, your life, the
  projects you have going, the people who matter, and what you want me to remember. Voice
  or text, as messy as you like." Ask at most two follow-ups, and only where a part would
  otherwise be empty.
- **Sources** are what they say. What you already remember fills gaps and checks names,
  and never leads. Don't read email, calendars, drives or trackers unless they ask.
- Take the last version they said, not the first ("well, co-run it" wins), and keep the
  why next to the fact it explains.

## Search first, then one proposal

1. **Search before you propose.** `ty search "<words>"` for each person, project and
   subject they mentioned, then `ty areas list`. Most of a ramble on an account that has
   data is already there in part.
2. **Submit ONE proposal** with `ty proposals create` (`references/commands-proposals.md`):
   - new details go on the topics and projects they already have, rather than new topics
     beside them;
   - file what is new into their existing areas, and propose a new area only for an
     ongoing part of their life that none covers;
   - mark each item's `source`: `conversation` for what they said in the ramble;
   - leave out what `references/memory.md` says never to keep, and ask before keeping
     what it says to ask about.
3. **Send the review link, not a list:** "Tell me when you've added it, or ask me to list
   it here." They untick what they don't want and click Add to TeamYou; nothing is saved
   before.
   Never write a ramble straight into TeamYou yourself.
4. **After they add it,** confirm in one line what was added, from the result.

Name anything that looks out of date or duplicated in the chat; never delete or rename
an item without their word.

## Where there is no review page

`ty proposals create` answers 404 on a deployment without the review page. There, say in
the chat, in a few lines, what you would add and where, and save it on their go, as
`references/memory.md` says ("Save well"). Read back what changed in one line.
